Simulation: each mode is a single-degree-of-freedom oscillator driven by independent white noise and discretized without approximation (zero-order hold), so its natural frequency and damping ratio are known exactly. Mode k has k half-waves along the girder, and all modes have the same response level.

Identification: output covariances up to time lag 2s−1 are assembled in a block Toeplitz matrix, whose singular vectors and values are obtained from the eigen-decomposition of T·TT. The system matrix follows from the shift structure of the observability matrix (Peeters & De Roeck, 1999). Stable poles are grouped into modes by single-linkage clustering on the relative frequency difference plus (1 − MAC). The bias is the identified minus the true damping ratio, averaged over all records computed with the current settings.

The computations are implemented in Python and NumPy and run in the browser through Pyodide. The SSI-cov implementation is taken from an operational modal analysis of the Hardanger Bridge (1310 m main span).
